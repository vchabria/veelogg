## Brand Commitments
User requests more white, brown, yellow and pink and real personal photos. Brand is Veelogg, Instagram @veelogg_. Prior user preferences identify Poppins as the main brand font, plain direct language, AI and automations as the subject, and no cringe. User offers also include websites, Shopify stores and apps.

The user rejected the first restrained version as too generic and explicitly requested a more fun experience, their existing handwritten logo, prominent free guides, iOS-style app icons, 21st.dev references, Readymag-style motion and SEO. The revision uses the original logo and app icon, bold Poppins, a functional six-item app dock, bounded movable real photos and a dedicated searchable hub for all eight published guides. The public 21st dock preview was studied; its locked component source was not accessed. Motion is implemented directly in React.

## Product Principles
- Describe the problem solved before tools or technical language.
- Let visitors compare scope and price before contacting.
- Preserve real personal identity and clear next steps.
- Use actual proof, with no invented results or urgency.
- Make free guides easy to find from the header, hero, app dock and homepage shelf.
- Keep playful interactions optional, with static touch and reduced-motion behavior.
- Keep this private review unindexed; enable indexing only as part of a public launch.
